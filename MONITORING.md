## Retraining and escalation triggers (rules)

1. Score PSI against the development sample above 0.25 on any monthly vintage: freeze automated use, open a review.
2. Score PSI between 0.10 and 0.25, or any core behavioural feature's CSI above 0.25: investigate within the cycle; check for data pipeline changes before touching the model.
3. Realized default rate in any score decile outside the calibration table's observed range by more than 5 percentage points for two consecutive vintages: recalibration review.
4. Test-style AUC on a fresh labelled vintage below the lower bound of the development bootstrap CI: full revalidation.
5. Approval-rate gap between any two audited demographic groups widening by more than 5 percentage points at a fixed cut-off: fairness review before the next deployment decision.
6. Any change to features, target definition, or portfolio mix: revalidation regardless of metric state (a change is a new model for governance purposes).

## Cadence

- Monthly: score PSI, per-feature CSI, decile calibration against realized outcomes, staging migration summary (from `data/credit.db` queries in `sql/queries.sql`).
- Quarterly: outcomes analysis (AUC, KS, Brier), fairness audit at the production cut-off, scenario ECL refresh.
- Annually, and on any trigger above: full revalidation in the VALIDATION.md structure, with the scorecard grade recomputed and every deduction named.

## The caveat that governs all of the above

PSI and CSI thresholds are conventions, not statistics with error guarantees (Yurdakul and Naranjo, 2020, Journal of Risk Model Validation). They allocate human attention. The numbers beside them, the calibration table, and the outcomes analysis are the evidence; the zones are only the alarm wiring.
